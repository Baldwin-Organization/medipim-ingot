THE SCRAPER

A sketchy source tries to glue two products together.

SECTION
JANUARYFEBRUARYSCRAPERHINDSIGHT

One scraper listing bridges both bottles. Merging established keys is gated, never automatic.

CLAIM scraper identity X-1 = {gtin:0111, gtin:0222} FLAG merge? SK_2 + SK_3 bridged — HELD for review RESOLVE merge SK_1 + SK_2 REJECTED (by alice)

Auto-merging would silently fuse the 250ml and 500ml products for every customer. Instead it sits in the queue until a human says yes — and a rejection is remembered too.
