The one idea

Nothing is ever overwritten.

Every statement a source makes is an immutable claim in an append-only log.
The golden record is just a pure function folded over that log:

fold(claims, priority)  →  golden

An EAN-13 and its GTIN-14 form are the same number at different widths.

Normalize before comparing — otherwise every format difference looks like a conflict between sources.
