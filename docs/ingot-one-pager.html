ingot · golden-record engine

Product identity as a replayable fold

Sources don't write rows — they assert claims about codes, kept forever with provenance. Identity, merges, survivorship, and history are all pure folds over one event log. Every answer the system gives can be re-derived from scratch, and diffed against what medipim does today.

decodelegacy deltas → contract-C envelope (per entity, per source)
claimsidentity / attribute / edge claims, dated, source-attributed
canonicalizeGTIN family → GTIN-14; shared codes partitioned out
clusterbridge on identity codes only — never on entity ids
reconcileledger mints keys; merges graded, splits tracked, suspects held
projectgolden record, as-of any date, API reads

The whole loop is Cluster.variants(Substrate.current(claims), shared) |> IdentityLedger.decide(…) — no GenServers, no runtime state, no dependencies. The log is the system of record; everything else is a projection.

Why it's built this way

Legacy ids are labels, not identity

Identity is re-derived from the codes themselves (CNK, GTIN, CIP…). Two listings that share a national code are the same product no matter what their entity ids say — and LegacyXref maps every old id to its new key as :stable, :split, or :merged.

The over-merge guard is structural

Bridge grades: national codes are trusted bridges, barcodes are suspect (ConflictFlagged, held for review), and shared codes — restricted GTINs, supplier refs, AU's ARTG numbers — ride along but never fuse two products.

Survivorship is a policy, not a schema

Conflicting values stay as candidates. A rank function injected at read time picks winners — MedipimPolicy reproduces the legacy SourcesRanker byte-for-byte; a market policy is a context map, not a module. No policy? The engine says needs_review instead of inventing an answer.

Time travel is free

Claims carry valid_from/recorded_at, so golden_as_of(date) answers "what did we believe on any given day" — including a replaced product still pointing at its successor the day before its identity died.

A new market is a data change

Field semantics live in one registry table (code_registry.ex). BE, FR, a books vertical, and now AU each landed as registry rows plus fixtures — the engine itself didn't change.

Cutover is diff-verified, not hoped

A parity harness replays real entities through the engine and medipim's applier semantics and diffs the outputs. Divergences are counted, classified, and pinned in tests. We migrate when the diff says so.
